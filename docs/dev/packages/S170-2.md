## Instructions
Local fixes. Do not touch ownership, refunds, rate limits, staging hosts, release storage or connect returns: those are in S170-1.

## Bugs (9)

### LS-05 (P2) Still open
- Repro: Open /pricing/.
- Expected: Renewal note matches renewal prices.
- Actual: Note says "Renew at 30% off: $34, $69 or $139"; renewal products are $39, $79, $159 (about 20%). The theme demo content repeats "30% off".
- Likely cause: Hard coded text in the page, not computed from products.
- Report: docs/dev/qa/2.3.3/license-server.md

### LS-09 (P3) Still open
- Repro: Export 10,000 licenses as CSV.
- Expected: A few seconds.
- Actual: 17.5 s (two extra queries per license); larger stores will time out.
- Likely cause: csv_rows() calls activations() and used_slots() per row (includes/class-sfls-licenses.php:767-787).
- Report: docs/dev/qa/2.3.3/license-server.md

### LS-10 (P3) Still open
- Repro: validate on a site that is not activated; activate with site_url=javascript:alert(1).
- Expected: Same error shape everywhere and a specific message.
- Actual: Not-activated answer is HTTP 200 with "message" while other errors use "error"; a bad site_url says "license_key and site_url are required."
- Likely cause: class-sfls-rest.php validate() and resolve().
- Report: docs/dev/qa/2.3.3/license-server.md

### LS-11 (P3) Still open
- Repro: Complete a license order; place a renewal order.

- Report: docs/dev/qa/2.3.3/license-server.md
